Play settings

Every letter is dealt once, in random order, so a full game is one surprise per letter (26 by default). After the last one, a finish screen shows all the letters. Space bar = next surprise. Replay (top right) shows the same one again. Tap the stage after the reveal to show or hide the answer letter. Use the letter buttons above to play with fewer letters.
